About me

Hi, I'm Sindura Reddy Challa. I recently completed my Master's in Data Science from the University of Maryland, Baltimore County. My journey so far has been shaped by curiosity, creativity, and a deep love for solving problems through technology. I'm always looking for new ways to improve, to learn, and to make a meaningful impact with the work I do.

For me, data isn’t just something to analyze. It’s a way of thinking that helps uncover patterns, ask better questions, and drive real change. I enjoy working with complex problems, whether that means building machine learning models, designing data pipelines, exploring large datasets, or turning messy information into something clear, reliable, and useful.

At WikiCharities, I worked on an AI-powered Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) chatbot built on Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases, enabling users to ask natural language questions and receive responses grounded strictly in verified documentation. My focus was on improving response reliability by combining AWS-managed embeddings with OpenSearch Serverless, explicitly returning “not documented” responses when information was unavailable to prevent hallucinations and support trust and safety objectives.

This work involved designing an AWS-native ingestion workflow to continuously update both structured and unstructured nonprofit content, from document ingestion and chunking to embeddings and vector indexing. I evaluated retrieval quality using a manually labeled set of representative queries, analyzing Top-K document presence before and after refinements. These improvements reduced unsupported responses by approximately 25% while improving overall response consistency.

Previously, as a Data Visualization & Reporting Analyst at the UMBC Career Center, I worked with student outcomes, enrollment patterns, and employer engagement data to support campus reporting and decision-making. Using SQL, I examined term-over-term trends, surfaced anomalies, and translated raw metrics into meaningful insights by combining quantitative analysis with qualitative input from career advisors and staff.

I communicated these insights through interactive Tableau dashboards and recurring reports designed for non-technical stakeholders, helping teams clearly understand patterns, evaluate programs, and make informed decisions.

During graduate school, I also served as a Student Orientation Advisor, supporting new and transfer students as they transitioned into university life at UMBC. Earlier, I worked as an Inventory Data Entry Assistant with Chartwells, where I assisted with inventory tracking and maintained detailed Excel records.

In my undergraduate studies in Information Technology, I took on leadership roles as Vice President of the SPIE Student Chapter, leading outreach initiatives that encouraged collaboration and technical exploration. I also served as a Student Placement Coordinator, acting as a bridge between the placement cell and the student community to help peers navigate the job search process.

One experience that significantly shaped my path was my apprenticeship with Knowledge Solutions India, where I developed a heart disease prediction model achieving 87% accuracy using logistic regression, decision trees, and random forests. Through feature engineering, hyperparameter tuning, and data visualization, I saw firsthand how data science can drive real-world impact, which inspired me to pursue a Master's in Data Science.

Outside of work and academics, I enjoy staying active through badminton and expressing creativity through photography and videography. Capturing moments through a lens feels therapeutic to me. If you're curious, you can explore some of my visual work on Instagram: @framebyframe06 .

Thanks for stopping by and taking a moment to learn more about me.
